A lot of [Minki](https://minki.cards)'s design is inspired by things I saw on some of my favorite
sites. I thought it would be interesting to compile <Pink>the original vs how it ended up featuring
in the end product!</Pink>

## Progress dock - Stripe Press

<MDXCompare
  leftSrc="/posts/stripe-press-dock.mp4"
  leftLabel="Stripe Press"
  rightSrc="/posts/minki-dock.mp4"
  rightLabel="Minki"
/>

[Stripe Press](https://press.stripe.com)'s navigation inspired my dock for navigating through a
card's pages, as well as <Pink>Apple's liquid glass.</Pink> I have a lens layer that redraws the dot
background at 1.42×, so the dots inside the panel are visibly bigger than the ones outside it, the
way they would be under real glass. It's pretty subtle but I really like the effect.

## CD - Design Spells

<MDXCompare
  leftSrc="/posts/design-spells-disc.mp4"
  leftLabel="Design Spells"
  rightSrc="/posts/minki-disc-card.mp4"
  rightLabel="Minki"

This came from [Design Spells](https://designspells.com), one of my favorite websites for UI
inspiration. I stumbled on this design for colorful discs that show the user's reflection. In Minki,
the discs show in the corner of your card and it plays a Spotify playlist.

I also added functionality to <Pink>write on it in marker like an actual burned CD.</Pink> You can
spin the disc while you're drawing, so the text curves the way it does when you're writing round
the edge of a real one:

## Loading screen - Figma

<MDXCompare
  leftSrc="/posts/figma-loading.mp4"
  leftLabel="Figma"
  rightSrc="/posts/minki-loading.mp4"
  rightLabel="Minki"
/>

I liked Figma's clean loading screen and created something similar, with a <Pink>staggered</Pink>
effect as the page loads. Each dot of the mark is delayed by its position in the logo, so it looks
like the whole thing is being drawn.

I saw this pretty looking vinyl record on Pinterest and it inspired me to <Pink>let people upload

almost directly from the Pinterest image's colors!

## Font - Nothing

<MDXCompare
  leftSrc="/posts/nothing-font.webp"
  leftLabel="Nothing"
  rightSrc="/posts/minki-font.webp"
  rightLabel="Minki"
/>

Minki's card font is [Ndot-47](https://fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/2047444), an open source
dot matrix face inspired by [Nothing](https://nothing.tech). The original only covers English and a
handful of accents (143 glyphs) <Pink>so I extended it with 243 more</Pink> to support a lot more
languages. It's also the font you're seeing on this site!
